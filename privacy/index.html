Privacy and Data Preferences

Updated September 16, 2026.

Analytics Preference

This preference applies to this browser. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable analytics automatically. Opting out removes the local analytics identifier; it does not delete previously recorded events or your game progress.

Game Progress

Games save scores, settings, and progress in browser localStorage. Play does not require an account. Clearing site data removes local progress and preferences. Downloaded pages may also be cached by a service worker for repeat visits; a first visit still requires a connection.

Optional Accounts and Public Scores

Google sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth. Signed-in game progress is backed up to Supabase. Your leaderboard score, display name, avatar, and account identifier are public; complete game backups and profile preferences are restricted to your account. Signing out preserves local progress on this device.

Avatar images can be loaded from their external image host. Hosting, authentication, and CDN providers receive connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests their services.

Usage Events

When enabled, the site sends a visit event once per tab session and a play event when a game page loads. Events include the game identifier, a server timestamp, and a persistent random browser identifier. A play event does not mean a completed game. New events do not store a signed-in account ID. Raw events are not publicly readable; aggregate statistics are available only to the site owner.

The visitor identifier is pseudonymous, not anonymous in an absolute sense. There is currently no automated retention schedule or self-service cloud account deletion screen. Do not assume clearing browser storage deletes server-side backups or historical analytics.

Data Requests

For account or data requests, contact the maintainer through vanshul.com. Do not post access tokens, private game backups, or identifying data in public GitHub issues. Requests may require verification of account ownership.
