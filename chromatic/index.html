About Chromatic — how to play & tips

How to play

Match the target colour using the red, green and blue sliders, then submit. Clear each round by getting close enough — the accuracy you need climbs with the difficulty. You have three lives in Endless mode; see how high you can go.

Tips

Adjust one channel at a time and watch the swatch shift. Warm targets want more red and green; cool ones lean blue. Hard mode asks for near-perfect matches.
