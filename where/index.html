About Where — how to play & tips

How to play

Where shows you a country's flag — or its capital city — and four country names. Tap the right one. Build a streak with correct answers; countries you miss come back later so they stick.

  • Flags: name the country from its flag.
  • Capitals: match the capital city to its country.
  • Easy covers 100 well-known countries; Hard adds the rest — nearly 200 in all.

Tips

Learn flags by their colours and symbols, then group countries by region — neighbours often share a palette. Your best score is saved on this device for each difficulty.
