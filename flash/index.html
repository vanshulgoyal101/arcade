About Flash — how to play & tips

How to play

Flash shows a passage one word at a time, centred on the optimal reading point so your eyes never move. Read it, then answer a quick comprehension question. Get it right and Flash speeds you up; miss it and it eases off.

Tips

Start around 300 words per minute and let the game push you. Trust your first instinct on the quiz — the aim is comprehension at speed, not perfect recall.
