About 2048 — how to play & tips

How to play

Every arrow key or swipe slides the whole board that way. Tiles showing the same number merge into their sum, and a fresh 2 (or occasionally a 4) drops into a free cell after any move that changed something. Reaching the 2048 tile wins — but you can keep playing for 4096 and beyond.

Tips

Choose a corner and never let your biggest tile leave it: if you keep it bottom-left, use only left and down, and reach for up as a last resort. Build a descending staircase along that edge so a single slide chains several merges at once. Keeping the board tidy matters more than chasing every merge — a full board with no matching neighbours is what ends the run.
