About Echo — how to play & tips

How to play

Watch the pads light up in sequence, then repeat the pattern back. Each round adds one more step and the tempo creeps up. Pick Forgiving to keep going after a slip, or Strict for a single life — and switch to six pads for a real challenge.

Tips

Say the pattern aloud or tap a rhythm as it plays. Chunking the sequence into small groups helps you hold much longer chains in memory.
