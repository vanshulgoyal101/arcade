About Digit Span — how to play & tips

How to play

A sequence of digits flashes on screen — then you type it back from memory. Each round adds one more digit. Forward mode repeats them in order; Reverse mode makes you type them backwards, which is much harder.

Tips

Group the digits into pairs or trios, like a phone number. Reverse mode is a real workout — start there only once forward feels comfortable.
